# V3XILITE — Public Roadmap

> This roadmap communicates direction, not proprietary implementation details or guaranteed release commitments.

## NOW — Foundations

Current development is focused on the foundations required for a trustworthy intelligence system:

- V3XILITE Proof core experience
- Accounts and identity
- Personal intelligence
- Conversations
- Projects
- Collaboration and decisions
- Controlled provider integration
- Evidence-aware workflows
- Mission foundations
- V3RA local-first intelligence research
- Specialized capability routing
- Verification and adversarial evaluation
- Privacy and isolation boundaries

## NEXT — Productization

The next public direction includes:

- deeper V3X Research workflows;
- governed V3X Code workflows;
- Mission Rooms;
- stronger Proof Receipts;
- broader Trust Fabric;
- V3X Operations;
- secure device/service control;
- expanded V3RA tools and presence;
- reusable verified results;
- richer personal and project continuity.

## LATER — Research direction

Longer-term exploration includes:

- Privacy Gateway / Vault;
- controlled capability evolution;
- broader multimodal presence;
- secure multi-channel interaction;
- stronger signed provenance;
- device and infrastructure orchestration;
- increasingly capable local-first intelligence;
- richer recovery and defensive analysis.

## Public target

**01.01.27**

The date is a development target for the public project direction. It should not be interpreted as a promise that every research component listed above will be generally available on that date.

## What we deliberately do not publish

This roadmap excludes:

- internal milestones and experiment identifiers;
- model sizes and private benchmarks;
- datasets and training recipes;
- prompts;
- routing thresholds;
- security rules;
- internal APIs;
- private infrastructure;
- implementation details that would materially assist replication or attack.

We would rather publish a smaller truthful roadmap than manufacture certainty.
